Privacy statement

Luggo is built to know as little about you as possible. Here’s exactly what we do and don’t collect.

Last updated 30 September 2026
1. Who we are

Luggo is a product of The RO Brand, Fokkerstraat 12, 3833 LD Leusden, The Netherlands (KvK 58083111, VAT NL852865867B01). We are the controller for the personal data described in this statement, and we process it in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. The app: no account, no location

You don’t need an account to use Luggo. The bags you pair — their names, types and beacon IDs — are stored only on your phone. Beacons tell your phone how close a bag is (Far, Medium, Near) and when it was last in range. Luggo does not determine, store or share the location of your bags or of you.

The app asks for Bluetooth permission to detect your beacons, and for notification permission to send in-range alerts. You can turn both off at any time in your phone’s settings or in the app.

3. Orders from our shop

When you order beacons, we process your name, delivery address, email address, phone number (if provided), order details and payment status. We use this to deliver your order, send order updates and handle returns or support questions. The legal basis is the performance of our contract with you. Payment details are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your full card details.

4. Who we share data with

Only with the parties we need to fulfil your order: our payment provider, our shipping partner and our hosting provider. They process data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

5. How long we keep it

Order data is kept for as long as needed to deliver and support your order, and for seven years afterwards where Dutch tax law requires us to keep financial records. Data in the app stays on your phone until you delete a bag or remove the app.

6. Website & cookies

This website only uses cookies that are needed for it to work, such as keeping your cart. We don’t use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Contact us to exercise any of these rights — we’ll respond within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

8. Changes & contact

We may update this statement when our product or the law changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions about privacy? Contact The RO Brand at Fokkerstraat 12, 3833 LD Leusden, The Netherlands, or call +31 6 45438943.
