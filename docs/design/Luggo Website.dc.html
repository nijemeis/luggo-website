HOW IT WORKS

From your pocket to the belt in three steps.

01
Pair in seconds

Hold a Luggo beacon against the back of your phone. The app finds it automatically — name it, pick the bag type, done.

02
Tuck it in your bag

Slim enough for any pocket, lining or tag sleeve. Check the bag as usual — the beacon quietly rides along.

03
Watch it come to you

At the belt, open Luggo. Each bag moves closer on screen, and you get a nudge the moment it’s in range.

TRY IT

Four zones. Zero guessing.

As a beacon’s signal gets stronger, Luggo moves the bag from Far to Medium to Near. Drag the slider to bring the suitcase in.
